This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. GitHub may keep this data in server logs for security purposes. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: there is a legitimate interest in delivering the website securely and reliably. GitHub, Inc. is certified under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Details are in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

No Cookies, Analytics or Tracking

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking tools. Fonts, icons and scripts are served from this website itself, so opening a page does not contact any other server.

If you switch between light and dark mode, your choice is saved in your browser’s local storage so the site remembers it. This setting stays on your device and is never sent to anyone.

Contact Form

If you use the contact form, the details you enter (name, email address and message) are sent to Formspree, Inc., a form service based in the United States, which forwards them to me by email. Formspree processes the data in the United States. Before sending, you are asked to consent to this. The legal basis is your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, and for the transfer to the United States, Art. 49(1)(a) GDPR. The United States may not offer a level of data protection equivalent to the EU; for example, US authorities may be able to access the data. You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future. Details on Formspree are in its privacy policy. I delete your message when it is no longer needed to deal with your enquiry, unless there is a legal obligation to keep it. If you prefer not to use the form, you can reach me through the profiles linked on the home page.

Links to Other Websites

This website links to other websites, such as GitHub, LinkedIn and Google Scholar. Those sites are only contacted when you follow a link, and their own privacy policies then apply.

Your Rights
